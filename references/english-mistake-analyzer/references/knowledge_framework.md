# 高中英语知识点框架

用于错题标签分类和薄弱点分析的知识体系。

## 一、词汇知识 (Vocabulary)

### 1.1 词性转换
- 名词 → 动词（e.g., success → succeed）
- 名词 → 形容词（e.g., beauty → beautiful）
- 动词 → 名词（e.g., decide → decision）
- 形容词 → 副词（e.g., quick → quickly）
- 形容词 → 名词（e.g., important → importance）
- 否定前缀（un-/in-/im-/il-/ir-/dis-/mis-/non-）

### 1.2 动词短语 (Phrasal Verbs)
- **take**: take off, take up, take in, take on, take over, take away, take down, take after
- **put**: put off, put on, put up, put down, put away, put out, put up with, put forward
- **get**: get on, get off, get over, get through, get along with, get away with, get rid of
- **come**: come up with, come across, come out, come on, come true, come into being
- **go**: go on, go over, go through, go out, go off, go against, go by
- **turn**: turn on, turn off, turn up, turn down, turn out, turn to, turn around
- **look**: look after, look for, look forward to, look up, look into, look down on
- **make**: make up, make out, make up for, make sense, make progress, make efforts
- **set**: set off, set out, set up, set aside, set about, set down
- **give**: give up, give in, give away, give out, give off, give back
- **call**: call on, call for, call off, call up, call at, call back
- **carry**: carry on, carry out, carry away, carry through
- **break**: break down, break out, break up, break into, break away from
- **hold**: hold on, hold up, hold back, hold out, hold one's breath
- **pick**: pick up, pick out, pick on

### 1.3 介词搭配
- 时间介词（in/on/at/during/for/since/by/until/before/after）
- 地点介词（in/on/at/to/into/onto/through/across/past/beside/beyond）
- 方式介词（by/with/through/in）
- 其他固定搭配（be interested in, be good at, be fond of, be tired of）

### 1.4 高频词汇辨析
- **affect vs effect**
- **accept vs except**
- **advice vs advise**
- **already vs yet vs still**
- **alone vs lonely**
- **arrive/reach/get to**
- **borrow vs lend**
- **bring vs take vs fetch vs carry**
- **rise vs raise vs arise vs arouse**
- **spend vs cost vs take vs pay**
- **say vs speak vs talk vs tell**
- **see vs watch vs look vs read**
- **wear vs put on vs dress vs have on**
- **win vs beat vs defeat**
- **join vs join in vs take part in vs attend**
- **happen vs occur vs take place**
- **damage vs destroy vs ruin**
- **divide vs separate**
- **search vs search for**

## 二、语法知识 (Grammar)

### 2.1 动词时态与语态
- 一般现在时
- 一般过去时
- 一般将来时（will / be going to / be to / be about to）
- 过去将来时
- 现在进行时
- 过去进行时
- 将来进行时
- 现在完成时
- 过去完成时
- 将来完成时
- 现在完成进行时
- 被动语态（各种时态的被动）
- 主动表被动（sell well, wash easily, need doing）

### 2.2 非谓语动词
- **动词不定式 (to do)**
  - 作主语、宾语、表语、定语、状语、补语
  - 省略to的情况（使役动词、感官动词后）
  - 不定式的时态和语态
  - 疑问词+不定式
- **动名词 (doing)**
  - 作主语、宾语、表语、定语
  - 只能接动名词的动词（enjoy, finish, avoid, consider, suggest, mind, practice, miss, risk, imagine, admit, deny, appreciate, escape, postpone）
  - 既可接不定式也可接动名词（意义不同：stop, remember, forget, regret, try, mean, go on）
- **分词**
  - 现在分词 (doing)：作定语、表语、宾补、状语
  - 过去分词 (done)：作定语、表语、宾补、状语
  - 独立主格结构

### 2.3 从句
- **名词性从句**
  - 主语从句
  - 宾语从句（that/if/whether/wh-引导）
  - 表语从句
  - 同位语从句
- **定语从句**
  - 关系代词（that/which/who/whom/whose/as）
  - 关系副词（where/when/why）
  - 限制性 vs 非限制性定语从句
  - 介词+关系代词
  - 只能用that的情况
- **状语从句**
  - 时间状语从句（when/while/as/before/after/since/until/as soon as/the moment）
  - 条件状语从句（if/unless/as long as/provided that/supposing that）
  - 原因状语从句（because/since/as/now that）
  - 让步状语从句（although/though/even if/even though/while/no matter wh-）
  - 目的状语从句（so that/in order that/for fear that）
  - 结果状语从句（so...that/such...that）
  - 方式状语从句（as/as if/as though）
  - 地点状语从句（where/wherever）
  - 比较状语从句（than/as...as/not so...as）

### 2.4 特殊句式
- **倒装句**
  - 完全倒装（here/there/up/down/in/out/now/then + 谓语 + 主语）
  - 部分倒装（否定词/only/so...that/虚拟条件句省略if）
- **强调句**（It is/was...that/who...）
- **省略句**
- **祈使句**
- **感叹句**
- **反意疑问句**

### 2.5 虚拟语气
- if虚拟条件句（与现在/过去/将来事实相反）
- 错综时间虚拟条件句
- 含蓄虚拟条件句（without/but for/otherwise/or）
- 名词性从句中的虚拟语气（suggest/demand/require/order/insist/propose等）
- wish/if only/as if/as though后的虚拟
- would rather/sooner后的虚拟
- It's (high) time后的虚拟

### 2.6 情态动词
- can/could
- may/might
- must
- shall/should
- will/would
- need/dare
- 情态动词+have done（对过去的推测/虚拟）

### 2.7 主谓一致
- 语法一致原则
- 意义一致原则
- 就近原则
- 特殊名词（集体名词、分数/百分数、量词短语、复数形式单数意义）

### 2.8 冠词
- 不定冠词 a/an
- 定冠词 the
- 零冠词
- 固定搭配中的冠词

### 2.9 代词
- 人称代词主格/宾格
- 物主代词（形容词性/名词性）
- 反身代词
- 指示代词（this/that/these/those/such/same）
- 不定代词（some/any/no/every/each/both/all/either/neither/none/other/another/much/many/few/little）
- it的用法（指代、形式主语/宾语、强调句）

## 三、语篇知识 (Discourse)

### 3.1 衔接手段
- 指称（人称指称、指示指称、比较指称）
- 替代（名词替代、动词替代、分句替代）
- 省略
- 连接（增补、转折、因果、时间）

### 3.2 逻辑关系词
- **增补**: and, also, besides, in addition, furthermore, moreover, what's more
- **转折**: but, however, yet, still, nevertheless, although, though, while, whereas, on the contrary, instead
- **因果**: because, since, as, for, so, therefore, thus, hence, as a result, consequently
- **举例**: for example, for instance, such as, like, take...for example
- **解释**: in other words, that is, namely
- **总结**: in conclusion, in short, in brief, to sum up, all in all
- **顺序**: first, second, then, next, finally, at last, afterwards
- **对比**: similarly, likewise, in the same way, compared with

## 四、知识点标签使用规范

### 标签粒度
- **一级标签**: 大类（如"词汇知识"、"语法知识"）
- **二级标签**: 中类（如"动词短语"、"非谓语动词"）
- **三级标签**: 具体知识点（如"take短语"、"动词不定式作宾语"）

### 标注原则
1. 一道错题可标注多个知识点标签
2. 优先标注导致错误的最直接知识点
3. 如知识点不明确，标注到二级标签即可
4. 保持标签一致性，使用本框架中的标准名称
